# TutinGo — Instagram Promo Film "Le prof vient à toi"

**Format:** 9:16 vertical · 1080×1920 · 25 fps · **48 s** · Instagram Reels (also cut to 15 s Story)
**Language:** French voice-over and supertitles, with Moroccan Darija touches. Arabic subtitle file is optional.
**Objective:** Parents in Morocco should understand in under 5 seconds that TutinGo sends a **verified private tutor to their home**, and should tap through to tutingo.com.
**Brand assets:** `tutingo-logo.png`, a black "Tutin" wordmark with a violet "Go" whose "o" is a map pin, and the tagline *Learn Anywhere*.

---

## 1. Creative concept

**"The pin that brings the teacher."**
The violet map pin from the logo is the hero device. It drops onto a Moroccan city map, travels through the streets like a paper cut-out, and lands on a family's door. The door opens and a tutor steps out of the pin. Each time the pin appears, the problem turns into the solution.

**Story arc (Problem → Pin → Proof → Promise):**
1. A stressed evening: homework chaos, a tired parent, traffic to tutoring centres.
2. The pin drops and TutinGo appears.
3. How it works in 3 steps: choose, book, the tutor comes home.
4. Proof: grades go up, the child is confident, the parent is relieved.
5. CTA: *Le prof vient à toi.* tutingo.com

**Style formula:** *Mixed-media editorial collage.* Flat 2D vector characters with risograph grain sit on real paper textures (graph-paper notebook, kraft paper, torn magazine). Zellige and arch shapes are used as graphic frames. Motion is a deliberate 12 fps "stepped" cut-out feel for characters, laid over smooth 25 fps camera moves and type.

### Palette (derived from the logo)
| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|

| Deep Violet | `#4B1FB8` | Shadows, gradients |

Rule: violet appears **only** on TutinGo-owned elements (pin, tutor's lanyard and bag, UI, CTA), so viewers learn that violet means TutinGo.

### Typography
- Headlines: **Poppins ExtraBold / Black**, all lowercase except the brand name. This matches the logo's geometric sans.
- Supertitles: Poppins SemiBold, 64–72 px, cream text on a violet tape-strip background.
- Arabic version: **Cairo Bold**.
